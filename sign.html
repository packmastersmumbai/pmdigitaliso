How it works
  1. Pick your name and draw your signature, then save.
  2. Open any document. Each signature box shows a small sign button.
  3. Clicking it places your signature and today's date in that box.
  4. Print or save as PDF — the buttons do not print, the signature does.
It stays on this device. The signature is held in this browser only. It is not uploaded, it is not visible to anyone else, and using a different browser or computer means drawing it again. Clearing your browsing data removes it.

A box that names someone else will not accept your signature — the button is disabled and says whose box it is. Nothing is ever signed automatically when a page opens.

This is a convenience for the electronic copy, to save retyping a name and a date. It is not a qualified electronic signature and does not replace the wet signature on the printed and issued copy.
